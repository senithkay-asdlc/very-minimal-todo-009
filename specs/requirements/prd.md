# Very Minimal Todo

## Problem Statement

People who want a simple personal list of things to do are burdened by feature-heavy todo tools. They want to jot down a task and tick it off, nothing more.

## Solution

A deliberately minimal todo app: a signed-in user adds todos and marks them as done. There is no editing, updating or deleting. Todos are stored persistently in a database.

## Actors

- User — a signed-in person who keeps their own todo list: adds todos and marks them done.
